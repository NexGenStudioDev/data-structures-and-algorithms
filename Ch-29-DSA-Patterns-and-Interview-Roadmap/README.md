## 🎯 The 14 Essential LeetCode Patterns
1. **Sliding Window:** Subarrays or substrings with target properties.
2. **Two Pointers:** Pair search in sorted arrays, palindrome checking, water trapping.
3. **Fast & Slow Pointers:** Cycle detection, linked list midpoint, happy numbers.
4. **Merge Intervals:** Overlapping ranges, scheduling, calendar conflicts.
5. **Cyclic Sort:** Numbers in range $1$ to $N$ with missing/duplicate elements.
6. **In-place Reversal of a LinkedList:** Reversing nodes in subsegments without extra memory.
7. **Tree BFS:** Level-order exploration, right/left side views, shortest paths in unweighted trees.
8. **Tree DFS:** Path sums, max path sum, diameter, subtree property validation.
9. **Two Heaps:** Median calculation, dynamic balance maintenance.
10. **Subsets & Backtracking:** Exhaustive combinations, permutations, partition searches.
11. **Modified Binary Search:** Rotated arrays, boundary queries, search on answer space.
12. **Top 'K' Elements:** Min-Heap / Max-Heap bounded tracking, Quickselect.
13. **K-way Merge:** Merging sorted arrays, matrices, or linked lists with a Min-Heap.
14. **0/1 Knapsack & Dynamic Programming:** Subproblem caching, decisions under constraints.
