# Code of Conduct

Learners and contributors of all experience levels are welcome. Be respectful, give specific feedback, and focus disagreements on the material or proposed change.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and sharing another person’s private information without permission are unacceptable.

If you experience or witness a problem, use GitHub’s reporting tools on the relevant content or contact a repository maintainer privately. Include a link and a short description. Keep sensitive details out of public issues.
